Three things every new player needs to know

Punch, don't panic

Trees, dirt, and stone all break by holding left-click. There's no wrong way to start, just start.

Night means danger

Zombies and skeletons spawn in the dark. Any shelter, even a hole in a wall, keeps you alive until morning.

Everything is a recipe

Wood becomes planks, planks become tools. The crafting table is the first thing worth building.
